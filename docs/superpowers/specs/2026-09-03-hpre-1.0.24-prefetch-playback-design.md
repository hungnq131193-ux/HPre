# HPre 1.0.24 Prefetch and Playback Design

## Muc tieu

Phat hanh HPre 1.0.24 de rut ngan thoi gian tu luc bam video den luc co khung hinh dau tien, tranh extraction trung lap, giu mot ExoPlayer duy nhat, cai thien startup buffering va sua che do Fill phu kin khung ma khong lam meo hinh.

Pham vi prefetch gom Home, Search va Related. Playlist, lich su va danh sach dang ky khong nam trong ban nay de han che extraction nen va nguy co rate-limit.

## Hien trang

- `VideoExtractionCoordinator` da gom cac yeu cau cung `ContentKey` vao mot extraction va cache `ExtractedVideoBundle` toi da 20 giay.
- `ExtractedVideoBundle` da chia se `VideoDetails`, `StreamInfo` va related videos, nen khong can extractor rieng cho metadata, stream va related cua cung video.
- `AppScopedPlayerControllerProvider` va `HPrePlaybackService` da giu mot controller va mot ExoPlayer pham vi ung dung/service.
- `WatchViewModel` hien tai lay stream song song voi metadata, nhung chua co prefetch tu danh sach va chua truyen thumbnail danh sach sang Watch de che player.
- Startup buffer hien la 1.000 ms; Auto chua ap tran 360p rieng trong giai do khoi dong.
- `FullScreenResizeMode.FILL` dang map sang `RESIZE_MODE_FILL`, co the keo gian hinh va khong tao center-crop phu kin nhu mong muon.

## Kien truc

### Mot extraction cho moi video

Giu luong extraction hien co thay vi tao repository hoac cache thu hai.

`VideoExtractionCoordinator` tiep tuc la noi so huu cache va in-flight extraction theo `ContentKey`. `NewPipeVideoService.video()`, `streamInfo()` va `related()` deu doc tu cung `ExtractedVideoBundle`. Mot lan prefetch, mo video, tai metadata hay tai related cung luc se cho cung ket qua; khong tao request extractor thu hai.

`forceRefresh` chi dung cho thao tac retry/recovery hien co. Prefetch khong force refresh va khong retry am tham khi extraction that bai.

### Cache stream ngan han

Moi entry luu bundle, thoi diem ghi va thoi diem het han hieu luc. Han hieu luc la gia tri som hon cua:

- 20 giay ke tu luc ghi.
- Timestamp expiry som nhat doc duoc tu URL stream/manifest, tru safety margin.

Parser chi chap nhan timestamp hop le va nam trong tuong lai. URL khong co expiry, expiry sai dinh dang hoac expiry khong dang tin cay se dung TTL 20 giay. Entry het han bi loai khi doc hoac khi them entry moi; cache van gioi han 16 entry theo LRU.

Khong gui HEAD, range probe hay bat ky yeu cau kiem tra URL rieng truoc playback. Media3 prepare truc tiep URL da extract. Recovery mot lan trong playback service van xu ly URL het han hoac loi mang sau prepare.

### Prefetch theo vung nhin thay

Home, Search va Related quan sat vi tri cac item dang hien thi bang `LazyListState.layoutInfo.visibleItemsInfo`.

Logic chon key nam trong mot ham thuan dung chung de ba man hinh co cung quy tac va co the unit test khong can Compose runtime.

Hanh vi:

- Khi danh sach co noi dung, chon video dang hien thi dau tien va toi da hai video ke tiep theo thu tu danh sach.
- Prefetch toi da ba key muc tieu, voi toi da hai extraction chay dong thoi.
- Key trung lap bi loai truoc khi submit; cache hit khong tao viec moi.
- Khi vung nhin thay thay doi, huy subscriber prefetch khong con nam trong tap muc tieu. Extraction chi bi huy khi khong con subscriber nao, de thao tac bam video co the tiep quan cung task.
- Prefetch chay trong application scope va khong chan main thread hay lam mat noi dung danh sach.
- Loi prefetch khong hien error UI va khong loop retry.

Khong prefetch media bytes, `MediaItem` hoac goi `prepare()` truoc thao tac bam.

### Luong mo video

Khi bam video:

1. Navigation truyen `ContentKey` va thumbnail URL san co sang Watch.
2. `WatchViewModel` goi `streamInfo()` ngay. Cache hit hoac in-flight hit tra bundle dung chung.
3. Khi co `StreamInfo`, goi `playerController.prepare()` ngay, khong doi metadata, history, related hoac comments.
4. Metadata tai sau va cap nhat tieu de, channel va cac thao tac. History chi ghi khi co details/progress hop le.
5. Related va comments giu gate sau startup player nhu hien co de khong tranh tai nguyen voi extraction va decoder khoi dong.

Ket qua tre cua video cu khong duoc prepare hoac cap nhat video moi; generation va cancellation guard hien co duoc giu.

## Player va chat luong

### Mot ExoPlayer

Khong tao player moi. `HPrePlaybackService` tiep tuc so huu ExoPlayer duy nhat; `AppScopedPlayerControllerProvider` chia se controller cho Watch, mini-player, nen, Picture-in-Picture va fullscreen. Chuyen video chi thay media source va prepare session moi.

### Startup buffer

`BUFFER_FOR_PLAYBACK_MS` giam tu 1.000 ms xuong 750 ms, nam trong khoang muc tieu 0,5-1 giay. Cac gia tri sau giu nguyen:

- Minimum playback buffer: 30 giay.
- Maximum playback buffer: 90 giay.
- Buffer sau rebuffer: 8 giay.
- Memory-aware target byte cap.

Day la thay doi toi thieu: startup nhanh hon trong khi buffer chong giat va gioi han bo nho hien co khong doi.

### Auto bat dau o 360p

Voi `UserQualityPolicy.Auto`, track selector ap tran video 360p truoc prepare. Khi Media3 bao `onRenderedFirstFrame` cho dung playback generation, bo tran startup va ap lai quality policy Auto cua nguoi dung de ABR tu nang chat luong theo bang thong.

Quality fixed do nguoi dung chon khong ap tran startup 360p va khong bi first-frame event ghi de. Audio-only khong ap dung tran video. Event tre tu media generation cu khong duoc thay doi policy cua session moi.

## Thumbnail va first frame

Watch nhan thumbnail URL tu item da bam va hien anh phu toan bo `PlayerSurface` voi center-crop/nen den phu hop. Neu details den truoc, thumbnail details co the thay URL danh sach neu URL ban dau trong.

Thumbnail chi bien mat khi:

- `hasRenderedFirstFrame` la true cho video hien tai.
- Audio-only dat `STATE_READY`.
- Playback ket thuc hoac co terminal error, de error UI khong bi che.

Khong dung fallback thoi gian 300 ms de an thumbnail video, vi player ready chua dam bao da render frame. Khi chuyen video, trang thai first-frame reset truoc prepare va thumbnail moi xuat hien ngay.

## Che do Fill

`FullScreenResizeMode.FILL` se map sang Media3 `RESIZE_MODE_ZOOM`: giu dung ty le, center-crop va cat phan thua de phu kin khung, khong con vien den.

Lua chon `ZOOM` rieng bi bo khoi enum/menu vi trung semantics moi cua Fill. Gia tri `ZOOM` da luu trong `SavedStateHandle` duoc doc thanh `FILL`; day la tuong thich can thiet voi state trong phien/restore. Picture-in-Picture van ep `FIT` de tranh crop ngoai y muon.

## Xu ly loi va an toan

- Cache chi luu extraction thanh cong.
- Cancellation cua prefetch khong map thanh loi UI.
- URL parser khong log URL, query, token, cookie hoac header.
- Expiry khong hop le fallback ve TTL ngan thay vi giu entry vo han.
- Playback error va recovery budget giu chinh sach hien tai; khong bypass login, age, geo, private, paid hoac rate-limit restriction.
- Khong them dependency, player, persistent stream cache hoac media prefetch.

## Kiem thu

### Unit

- Nhieu caller cung `ContentKey` chi chay mot extraction va chia se bundle.
- Prefetch va `streamInfo()` cung key chia se in-flight task.
- Cache eviction dung expiry URL som hon TTL, safety margin va fallback TTL 20 giay.
- URL expiry malformed/past khong tao cache entry song qua TTL cho phep.
- Prefetch loai key trung, toi da ba target va hai extraction dong thoi; thay doi viewport huy subscriber cu an toan.
- Home, Search va Related chon item visible dau tien cung toi da hai item ke tiep.
- `WatchViewModel` prepare ngay khi stream ready, khong doi details/history/related/comments.
- Auto ap 360p khi startup va bo tran dung first frame/generation; Fixed va audio-only khong bi thay doi.
- Startup buffer la 750 ms, rebuffer/min/max buffer giu nguyen.
- Thumbnail giu den first frame, reset khi doi video va khong che terminal error/audio-only ready.
- `FILL` map center-crop, persisted `ZOOM` map sang `FILL`, menu khong con lua chon trung.
- Version la `1.0.24`, code 25.

### Build va thiet bi

- Chay unit test, lint, debug build, release build va `git diff --check`.
- Chay `UpstreamSmokeTest` va `LivePlaybackGateTest` tren thiet bi/emulator ket noi.
- Thu Home, Search va Related: scroll, bam item dang prefetch va item chua prefetch; xac nhan khong extraction trung lap va playback bat dau.
- Thu Auto tren mang on dinh: first frame toi da 360p, sau first frame ABR co the nang chat luong.
- Thu fullscreen Fill voi video 16:9 tren man hinh co ty le khac: khong vien den, khong meo hinh, chap nhan cat mep.
- Thu thumbnail khong tao man den truoc first frame.

## Phien ban va release gate

- `versionName = "1.0.24"`.
- `versionCode = 25`.
- Baseline la APK `HPre-v1.0.23-release.apk` tu GitHub Release chinh thuc.
- Candidate phai ky bang release key HPre cu va co certificate SHA-256 trung baseline.

Thu tu phat hanh:

1. Tat ca unit test, lint, debug/release build va `git diff --check` PASS.
2. Upstream smoke va playback gate PASS tren thiet bi/emulator.
3. `apksigner` xac minh candidate va certificate trung APK 1.0.23.
4. `verify-android-upgrade.ps1` PASS tu 1.0.23 sang 1.0.24, gom `adb install -r` va bao toan du lieu.
5. Commit code, day `main` len `origin`, tao tag `v1.0.24`, tao GitHub Release va upload APK da ky.
6. Xac minh release URL va asset APK cong khai.

Neu thieu thiet bi/emulator, signing credential, GitHub CLI/auth hoac quyen push, dung tai gate tuong ung va bao blocker. Khong tao tag/release hay tuyen bo da phat hanh khi gate chua PASS.

## Ngoai pham vi

- Prefetch playlist, lich su va subscription feed.
- Persist `StreamInfo` qua process restart.
- Prefetch media byte hoac prepare player truoc thao tac bam.
- Thay playback engine, them player, them dependency hoac viet lai recommendation pipeline.

`ponytail:` Chi prefetch toi da ba item voi concurrency hai va TTL toi da 20 giay. Nang cap thanh adaptive window/TTL chi khi telemetry cho thay cache miss hoac extraction latency van la nut that lap lai ma khong lam tang rate-limit.
