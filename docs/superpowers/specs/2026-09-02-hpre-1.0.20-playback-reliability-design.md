# HPre 1.0.20 Playback Reliability Design

## Muc tieu

Phat hanh HPre 1.0.20 de uu tien kha nang phat video on dinh qua manifest adaptive va tu phuc hoi mot lan khi nguon Media3 loi co the do URL tam thoi, mang, hoac source delivery.

Video van phu thuoc vao NewPipeExtractor va upstream public. Ban phat hanh khong duoc hua phat moi noi dung, bo qua han che truy cap, hoac che giau loi upstream.

## Bang chung va pham vi

- `v1.0.19` chi tang version; khong co thay doi playback so voi `v1.0.18`.
- `StartupStreamSelector` hien uu tien HLS/DASH truoc direct progressive/merged A/V.
- Fallback hien tai chi xu ly loi codec/container. `401`, `404`, `5xx` va network timeout khong co source fallback; `403` chi refresh URL mot lan.
- `VideoOpenMetrics` chi bat trong debug, nen APK release khong co du lieu an toan de tach do tre extraction, prepare, ready va first frame.
- Chua co thiet bi/emulator ket noi tai thoi diem dieu tra; live upstream va Media3 decode gate phai duoc chay truoc phat hanh.

## Thiet ke

### Lua chon stream khi mo video

`StartupStreamSelector` uu tien HLS, sau do DASH, truoc progressive/merged A/V cho ca VOD va livestream. Live Media3 gate voi query duoc duyet da cho thay 5 direct candidate tra `StreamExpired` sau prepare, trong khi manifest source dat ready, first frame, advance va seek. Media3 giu nguyen user quality policy; nguoi dung co the doi chat luong sau khi video bat dau phat.

Khi khong co manifest hop le, selector giu progressive, merged A/V va audio-only fallback hien co. Livestream giu HLS/DASH de bao toan live-edge va ABR.

### Phuc hoi playback

`HPrePlaybackService` theo doi source type da thu va mot ngan sach recovery cho tung playback session. Khi Media3 bao loi co the phuc hoi:

- `403` refresh `StreamInfo` mot lan, nhu hien tai.
- `401`, `404`, `5xx`, network connection failure va timeout cung duoc refresh mot lan vi URL signed, edge/CDN va ket noi co the thay doi giua extraction va prepare.
- Khong auto-refresh `LoginRequired`, `ContentUnavailable`, `AgeRestricted`, `GeoRestricted` hay `RateLimited` khi loi da map ro rang.
- Khi refresh thanh cong, chon source chua thu. Neu khong co source moi, dung source selector thong thuong tu `StreamInfo` moi.
- Khi refresh khong thanh cong hoac ngan sach session da het, cong bo AppError hien co; khong lap retry vo han.

Moi recovery giu ContentKey, vi tri VOD, live-edge cho livestream, y dinh play/pause, playback speed va quality preference neu con phu hop. Player van chi co mot owner la `HPrePlaybackService`.

### Telemetry an toan

`VideoOpenMetrics` co sink bat theo build de APK release phat record tong hop an toan. Record chi gom milestone, elapsed milliseconds, generation va source/error category da map. Khong duoc log query, ContentKey/native ID, URL, header, cookie, token, response body hay raw exception message.

Milestone giu `VIDEO_OPEN_START`, extraction start/finish, stream ready, player prepare/ready va first frame. Playback error them loai source va AppError da map de phan biet extractor, network, URL expiry va decoder/container failure.

### Phien ban va release

- `versionName = "1.0.20"` va `versionCode = 21`.
- APK release phai ky bang certificate HPre cu, trung certificate SHA-256 voi APK 1.0.19.
- Baseline upgrade la APK `HPre-v1.0.19-release.apk` tu GitHub Release chinh thuc.

## Xu ly loi va an toan

- Khong bypass age, geo, private, paid, login hay rate-limit restrictions.
- Chi retry mot recovery per playback session; source fallback cung khong quay lai source type da thu.
- Cancellation, transition sang video khac va service release huy recovery dang cho; late result khong duoc prepare session cu.
- Refresh failure giu loi da map; raw upstream detail khong vao UI hay telemetry.
- Direct-first khong loai bo HLS/DASH fallback va khong thay doi cache, PiP, background playback, history hay fullscreen semantics.

## Kiem thu

### Unit

- HLS/DASH hop le duoc chon truoc direct stream cho VOD va livestream; direct/audio fallback giu nguyen khi manifest khong co.
- Live gate dung source theo runtime policy; manifest xac nhan ready, first frame, advance va seek, con direct source xac nhan quality rebuild khi co alternate quality.
- Recovery eligibility bao phu `403`, `401`, `404`, `5xx`, connection failure va timeout; loai tru access/restriction/rate-limit errors.
- Recovery refresh toi da mot lan per session, giu vi tri, play intent, speed, quality va khong dung source type da thu.
- Failure, cancellation va chuyen session khong de recovery cu ghi de session moi.
- Release telemetry redact ContentKey, URL, token, header, cookie va raw exception; van co milestone, elapsed time va category.
- Version test xac nhan 1.0.20/code 21.

### Thiet bi/upstream

- Chay `UpstreamSmokeTest` voi query duoc duyet `Kotlin tutorial`; khong luu query hay candidate IDs/URLs trong artifact.
- Chay `LivePlaybackGateTest` voi cung query tren thiet bi/emulator: surface attach, ready, rendered first frame, advance, seek va quality switch.
- Thu video VOD progressive, merged A/V va manifest neu upstream tra ve; xac nhan source fallback va refresh khong loop.
- Do it nhat nam lan cold/warm tren cung thiet bi, mang va candidate. Bao median cho tap-to-prepare va tap-to-first-frame; khong cong bo phan tram neu khong co paired evidence.

## Release gate

1. Tat ca unit test, lint, debug/release build va `git diff --check` PASS.
2. Upstream smoke va live playback gate PASS tren thiet bi/emulator ket noi.
3. APK release duoc ky bang certificate HPre cu; `apksigner` xac nhan certificate trung baseline 1.0.19.
4. `verify-android-upgrade.ps1` PASS tu APK 1.0.19 sang candidate 1.0.20, gom `adb install -r` va bao toan du lieu.
5. Day source len GitHub, tao tag `v1.0.20`, tao GitHub Release va upload APK chi sau cac gate tren PASS.

Neu khong co thiet bi/emulator, release credentials, hoac GitHub CLI/auth, dung truoc gate tuong ung va bao blocker. Khong tuyen bo da phat hanh.

## Ngoai pham vi

- Prefetch media byte, media item hoac `prepare()` truoc thao tac nguoi dung.
- Player thu hai, playback subsystem moi, dependency moi, hoac doi ownership cua player.
- Thay doi buffer policy, cache size, extraction cache, ABR, PiP, background playback, history va giao dien khong phuc vu recovery.

`ponytail:` Khong dat timeout toan cuc ngan hon cho extraction hay playback. Nang cap thanh adaptive deadline theo telemetry chi khi du lieu release an toan cho thay mot pha co latency bat thuong lap lai.
